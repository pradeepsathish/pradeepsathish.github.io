Music doesn't come with a manual.

ISMIR 2026 · Late-Breaking/Demo · Sole Author

A song isn't labeled “melancholic” or “D minor.” It's sound — a waveform, a spread of frequencies, a harmonic shape repeating over time. ALAI reads it that way instead of relying on tags, and the genre-conditioned key-detection method behind it is the subject of the paper below.
